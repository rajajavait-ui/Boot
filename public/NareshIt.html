The Board for Control of Cricket in India (BCCI) has called a crucial meeting with the men’s team management in Raipur on Wednesday, aiming to reset the roadmap across formats and address emerging faultiness in communication and selection. The meeting will take place on match day, ahead of the second ODI between India and South Africa, and is designed to bring clarity at a time when questions are mounting over tactics in Test cricket and the relationship of the management with the senior players.
