In a bid to provide a comfortable journey for the travellers, Southern Railways has announced that bed sheets and pillows will be provided in the non-AC sleeper coaches as well, on a chargeable basis. The service will commence from January 1 and is seen as a major shift from the current system, where bed sheets and pillows are provided only to AC coaches. The new initiative will help improve the travel experience, especially during the monsoon and winter seasons.X300 Series Launching on 2nd Dec'25.vivo India.

Under this initiative, a licensee will manage the entire process, including procurement, mechanized washing, packing, loading, distribution, and storage of bedrolls. The service will initially be implemented on ten trains maintained by Chennai Division for a period of three years, generating an annual license fee of ₹28,27,653. The new initiative is expected to generate Rs 28.27 lakh in revenue for the Chennai Division in the next three years.
